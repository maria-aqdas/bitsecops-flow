What Upkeep does

A repository never stays clean on its own. Upkeep runs Bob 2.0 on a fixed cadence, turns whatever it finds into a scored, auditable backlog, and only auto-ships the fixes a developer would have approved anyway.

1

Scan on a schedule

Bob reads the whole repository, not just the latest diff, so debt from six months ago surfaces too.

2

Score before touching anything

Every item gets a risk and effort score. Nothing is fixed until it's been classified.

3

Fix in parallel, verify every time

Safe items go to subagents at once; each fix is re-tested before it's marked done.

Without Upkeep

Debt gets found by accident — during an outage, a slow PR review, or a new hire asking why a function still exists. By then it's expensive to fix.

With Upkeep

Bob scans on schedule, classifies each item by risk and effort, fixes the safe ones in parallel, and hands the rest to a developer with full context attached.
